MILLENNIAL JOINT: REMASTERED

Millennial Joint: Remastered is a remastered version of the original Millennial Joint. The same quirky, millennial-themed burger joint is back, but this time everything has been rebuilt to look better, play smoother, and feel more responsive. Grill, plate, and serve trendy burgers, fries, and drinks while juggling lines of hungry customers, burning food, and order combos across brand-new maps.

• ABOUT THE REMASTER

I remastered this version solo, taking the original class project and making everything better and smoother. I added joystick support, customers who line up to get their orders, food that can burn, order combos, new maps, better controls, more SFX and VFX, smart ingredient suggestions so players have an easier time, a settings screen before starting to adjust the gameplay, and a lot more. The original team is credited on the Millennial Joint page.
