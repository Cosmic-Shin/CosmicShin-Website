VOCAL STRIKE

Vocal Strike is a fast-paced 2D space shooter where you pilot your favorite Vocaloid idol through a neon-drenched cosmic battlefield. Choose between Hatsune Miku and Kasane Teto, dodge relentless waves of bullets, chain your movement through chaotic enemy patterns, and unleash dazzling musical attacks against bizarre bosses inspired by internet legends, meme culture, and pure celestial chaos.

ENGINE C#
TIMELINE 7 DAYS
PLATFORM PC
• MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTIONS

Lead Developer & 2D Artist: Designed and built the project from the ground up, implementing the combat loop, input handling, enemy behavior, boss phases, and visual polish. I also handled the game’s 2D art direction, UI styling, and overall presentation to match the absurd, high-energy vibe of an idol-driven cosmic arena battle.

• PROJECT ORIGIN & DESIGN VISION

Built during a seven-day game jam, this project merges the intensity of a classic arcade shooter with the colorful exuberance of Vocaloid fandom. The goal was to create a compact but memorable game that blends energetic combat, bold character identity, and chaotic boss encounters into a single, fast and playable package under a tight production schedule.
