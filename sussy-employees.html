SUSSY EMPLOYEES

Set in a bustling modern office, Sussy Employees turns a familiar workplace into a playful arena for tasks, movement, and stealth sabotages. Inspired by classic pixel titles like Kirby, the game features vibrant, chibi-style TV characters with soft shapes and expressive faces. Players navigate a cleanly designed pixel-art office layout filled with interactive props, creating a colorful and charming space for high-stakes multiplayer deceit.

• MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTIONS

Lead Developer & Pixel Artist: Handled end-to-end technical development, online multiplayer networking pipelines, character movement, task logic, and stealth sabotage mechanics. Additionally, I crafted custom 2D UI layouts, HUD elements, and original pixel-art game assets, including character customizer menus for TV head outfits.

• PROJECT ORIGIN & DESIGN VISION

Developed as a major coursework assignment testing our capability to engineer a fully functional online multiplayer game. Taking inspiration from Among Us, my team and I chose to replace the standard space setting with a cozy modern office populated by customisable TV-headed characters that players can dress up in various outfits while working together—or secretly betraying each other.
