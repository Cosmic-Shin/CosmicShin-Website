MILLENNIAL JOINT

Millennial Burger Joint Simulator is a fast-paced cooperative cooking game set in a vibrant, quirky restaurant where players work together to prepare and serve trendy burgers, fries, and drinks. The game combines time management, coordination, and humor as players grill, plate, and deliver dishes while dealing with chaotic kitchen hazards and eccentric customers. Its unique millennial aesthetic features oversized burgers with knives on top, fries served on newspaper, and playful, whimsical environments that make cooking feel both fun and frantic.

• MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTIONS

Solo Developer & Lead Programmer: Handled the entire core architecture, game loops, local multiplayer inputs, and player interaction systems. In addition to coding, I designed and implemented custom vector UI components, HUD layout elements, and menu navigation flows tailored to keep fast-paced kitchen chaos readable.

• PROJECT ORIGIN & DESIGN VISION

Developed as a class assignment focused on demonstrating our capability to engineer a fully functional local multiplayer title packed with unexpected gameplay twists. Taking heavy inspiration from cooperative party games like Overcooked, my team and I crafted a high-energy cooking simulator where players can either collaborate to fulfill orders or betray one another through sabotage, dynamic kitchen hazards, and shifting customer demands.
