VISITOR STATS

To see how many people visit and which games they look at, this site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visitor counter. It doesn't use cookies or save anything in your browser, and it doesn't keep personal information like your IP address. It records the page you viewed, the site that sent you here, your browser, screen size and country, plus clicks on the download and play buttons.

SAVED ON YOUR DEVICE

Your site settings (theme, mouse trail, background stars, CRT effect, custom cursor, sound effects and game volume) are saved in your browser's local storage so they stay the same between visits. They never leave your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser resets them.

Your achievement progress is saved the same way. It stays in your browser and is never shared or uploaded.

If you play Shitty Shift in the browser, Unity may also save the game files on your device so the game loads faster next time.
