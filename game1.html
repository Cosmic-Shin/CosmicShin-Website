SHITTY SHIFT

Shitty Shift is a first-person 3D psychological horror-puzzle game set within the silent, suffocating corridors of an abandoned school during a late-night janitorial shift. The story follows a worn-out janitor who regains consciousness on the restricted third floor after falling victim to a malicious hallucinogenic prank. Upon waking, the familiar school transforms into a surreal nightmare—corridors warp, exits seal off, and grotesque creatures forged from decaying trash and school waste stalk the shadows. To escape the lock-in, players must manage their fragile sanity, scavenge for vital tools, and solve multi-layered environmental puzzles while evading relentless, trash-born monstrosities hunting them through the dark.

• MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTIONS

Lead Developer & System Programmer: Spearheaded full technical development, implementing core gameplay mechanics, puzzle logic, state management, and player interaction pipelines. Additionally, I modeled custom 3D environmental props, crafted UI/HUD elements, and balanced atmospheric lighting to drive player tension.

• PROJECT ORIGIN & DESIGN VISION

Created as a core coursework project designed to test our ability to scope, build, and ship a fully playable, mechanics-driven puzzle game within a tight 1-month development sprint. Drawing deep artistic and structural inspiration from psychological horror titles like Silent Hill f, my team and I aimed to merge eerie, claustrophobic school settings with environmental puzzle-solving and relentless waste-monster mechanics.
