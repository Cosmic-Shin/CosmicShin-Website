LUCK OF SPEED

Luck of Speed is a fun racing game where anything can happen! Grab boxes on the track to get random effects—some will help you, some might slow you down. Finish first to earn money and upgrade your car to go even faster. Outsmart your opponents, avoid obstacles, and enjoy the chaos on every race. How lucky are you on the track?

ENGINE C#
TIMELINE 1 MONTH
PLATFORM PC
• MY ROLE & CONTRIBUTIONS

Solo Developer: Built the full racing loop, car controller, opponent behavior, random box effects, progression system, and core gameplay balance in C#. All assets used in the project were not mine, but I handled the implementation, gameplay systems, and overall design direction.

• PROJECT ORIGIN & DESIGN VISION

This was a class project designed to test our capability in handling 3D physics, vehicle movement, and arcade-style competition under a short production timeline. The goal was to build a fast, chaotic racing game that rewarded improvisation, momentum control, and a bit of luck on every lap.
